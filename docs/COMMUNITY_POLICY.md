# MALTY Community Reserve Policy

## Purpose

The Community Reserve exists to support documented community programs, participation initiatives, educational/community activities and other transparent uses that are consistent with the MALTY project purpose.

This policy defines how the Community Reserve is accounted for and how planned community allocations should be documented. It does not execute token transfers or guarantee any financial return.

## Allocation

- Total Community Reserve: **150,000,000 MALTY**.
- Planned initial availability: **up to 25,000,000 MALTY**.
- Planned remaining reserve after the initial launch allocation: **125,000,000 MALTY**.
- Reserve wallet: `MALTY Community`.
- Public address: `BFYWFeqf8uJ4BvD6dfySCkdMVdtDPvxFe5hmSzuCCbhg`.

The planned initial amount is a maximum allocation, not an automatic distribution. Until tokens are actually distributed for a documented purpose, they remain reserve assets and should not be reported as circulating solely because the amount is planned.

## Approved Community Uses

Community Reserve tokens may be considered for:

- documented community campaigns;
- participation or contribution programs;
- educational initiatives;
- community events and activities;
- transparent rewards for defined project contributions;
- other community purposes approved under the project's governance process.

Each program should define its purpose, eligibility or participation rule, planned amount, distribution method and relevant public transaction references where appropriate.

## Distribution Principles

Community distributions should follow these principles:

1. **Transparency:** the project should document the purpose and amount before a material distribution.
2. **Consistency:** equivalent participants should be treated under the same published rules where practical.
3. **No guaranteed return:** community allocations are participation/community resources, not promises of profit or appreciation.
4. **No hidden allocation:** undisclosed transfers from the Community Reserve should not be represented as community distributions.
5. **Reconciliation:** every material movement should be reconciled against the Community Reserve ledger.

## Initial Community Allocation

The current tokenomics plan allows up to **25,000,000 MALTY** to become available for initial community activity. The amount actually distributed may be lower depending on the launch plan and documented programs.

Until a specific program is approved and executed, the project should report this amount as **planned initial availability**, not as already distributed tokens.

## Recipient Handling

For individual or group distributions, the project should collect only the information required to identify the destination wallet and verify the published distribution rules. Private keys, seed phrases and recovery credentials must never be requested, stored or committed to the repository.

## Governance and Reallocation

A movement that changes the Community Reserve allocation itself, rather than simply distributing tokens for an approved community purpose, must follow the project's tokenomics Change Governance policy.

Material changes to this policy should be documented in the repository before implementation where practical.

## Circulating Supply Treatment

A Community Reserve token becomes part of reported circulating supply only when it has actually been distributed or otherwise made available according to the project's documented circulation methodology.

A planned allocation, an internal accounting entry or an unused reserve balance does not by itself create circulating supply.

## Current Status

The 150,000,000 MALTY Community allocation and the up-to-25,000,000 MALTY initial availability remain planned tokenomics figures until corresponding on-chain movements occur.

## Reconciliation Record

For each material Community Reserve movement, record:

- date/checkpoint;
- balance before movement;
- amount moved;
- community program or purpose;
- destination public address or transaction signature where appropriate;
- resulting reserve balance;
- circulating-supply impact, if any.
