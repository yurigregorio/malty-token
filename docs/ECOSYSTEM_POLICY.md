# MALTY Ecosystem Reserve Policy

## Purpose

The Ecosystem Reserve exists to support the long-term development, launch support, integrations, partnerships, and ecosystem activities of MALTY.

This document defines the intended allocation and transparency rules for the reserve. Planned allocations are not considered circulating until the tokens are actually distributed or otherwise made available.

## Allocation

- **Total supply:** 1,000,000,000 MALTY
- **Ecosystem Reserve:** 150,000,000 MALTY (15% of total supply)
- **Initial amount potentially available:** up to 17,500,000 MALTY (1.75% of total supply)
- **Remaining reserve after the initial planned availability:** 132,500,000 MALTY

The Ecosystem Reserve was reduced from 200,000,000 MALTY to 150,000,000 MALTY so that 50,000,000 MALTY (5% of total supply) could be assigned to the dedicated MALTY Impact Reserve. Total supply remains unchanged.

## Approved Uses

The Ecosystem Reserve may be used for documented ecosystem purposes, including:

- Partnerships and integrations
- Launch and ecosystem support
- Development-related ecosystem initiatives
- Marketing and community-facing initiatives that support ecosystem growth
- Infrastructure or service costs directly related to the project
- Strategic ecosystem initiatives approved under the project's governance process

Animal-welfare allocations intended for MALTY Impact should use the dedicated MALTY Impact Reserve rather than the Ecosystem Reserve.

## Transparency Rules

1. Planned allocations must not be presented as already distributed.
2. Material movements from the Ecosystem Reserve should be documented with date, amount, destination, purpose, and transaction reference when applicable.
3. No hidden allocation or undisclosed compensation should be represented as an ecosystem distribution.
4. The reserve does not guarantee any return, price appreciation, or financial outcome.
5. Any change to the allocation should be documented and reconciled against the total supply.
6. The initial 17,500,000 MALTY amount is a planning limit, not an automatic transfer or circulating-supply event.

## Current Status

Actual on-chain balances and movements are maintained in `docs/RECONCILIATION.md`. The policy allocation itself must not be treated as proof that the full amount has already been transferred to the reserve wallet.

## Security

This policy intentionally contains public wallet information only. No private key, seed phrase, recovery phrase, or signing secret should ever be committed to GitHub or included in project documentation.
