# MALTY Team Reserve Policy

## Purpose

The Team & Core Contributors Reserve exists to support documented long-term project development and contributor-related needs. It is intended to align team incentives with continued development and operation of MALTY without creating an undisclosed or automatic circulating allocation.

Planned allocations are not considered circulating until tokens are actually distributed or otherwise made available.

## Intended Uses

The Team Reserve may support documented project-related purposes, including:

- Long-term contributor incentives
- Compensation or rewards for documented project contributions
- Development and maintenance activities
- Technical, operational, or product work directly supporting MALTY
- Other team-related purposes approved through the project's governance process

## Release Policy

1. Up to **10% (7.5M MALTY)** may initially be eligible for distribution.
2. The remaining **90% (67.5M MALTY)** is intended to be released in **12 equal monthly tranches**.
3. Eligibility does not itself make tokens circulating.
4. Actual distributions must be documented and reconciled.
5. The technical enforcement mechanism is **TBD** until a verifiable on-chain or custody mechanism is selected.
6. The Team Reserve must not be represented as an on-chain lock or vesting contract until such a mechanism actually exists.
7. Any change to the release schedule must be documented and reconciled against the total supply.

## Transparency

The Team Reserve does not guarantee any return, price appreciation, or financial outcome. Its purpose is operational and incentive alignment rather than a promise of value.

Where team tokens become transferable or distributed, the project should maintain a clear public record of the relevant allocation and transaction information.
