# MALTY Team Reserve Policy

## Purpose

The Team Reserve exists to support documented long-term project development and contributor-related needs. It is intended to align team incentives with the continued development and operation of MALTY without creating an undisclosed or automatic circulating allocation.

Planned allocations are not considered circulating until tokens are actually distributed or otherwise made available.

## Intended Uses

The Team Reserve may support documented project-related purposes, including:

- Long-term contributor incentives
- Compensation or rewards for documented project contributions
- Development and maintenance activities
- Technical, operational, or product work directly supporting MALTY
- Other team-related purposes approved through the project's governance process

## Release Principles

1. Team allocations should follow the project's documented release schedule.
2. A planned team allocation must not be represented as already distributed.
3. Material distributions should be documented with date, amount, destination, purpose, and transaction reference when applicable.
4. Undisclosed or hidden team distributions should not be represented as community or ecosystem allocations.
5. Team tokens should not be described as permanently locked unless a verifiable locking mechanism exists.
6. Any change to the release schedule should be documented and reconciled against the total supply.

## Transparency

The Team Reserve does not guarantee any return, price appreciation, or financial outcome. Its purpose is operational and incentive alignment rather than a promise of value.

Where team tokens become transferable or distributed, the project should maintain a clear public record of the relevant allocation and transaction information.
